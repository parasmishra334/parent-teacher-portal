Comprehensive Feature Suite

Everything You Need for School Collaboration

A streamlined, role-based platform designed with Indian schools and families in mind.

Academic Progress & Grades

Subject-wise marks breakdown, grading system (A1, A2, B1), cumulative progress meters, and personalized teacher comments for every assessment.

Daily Attendance Tracking

Interactive calendar and table views showing daily status (Present, Absent, Late) with automatic overall attendance percentage calculation.

Fee Payments & Invoices

Track tuition, activity, and transport fee schedules. Simulated instant checkout turns pending fees to Paid with printable payment receipts.

Direct Messaging System

Secure school communications with Inbox, Outbox, Compose, unread badge alerts, and direct replies between parents and teachers.

PTM Meeting Scheduler

Parents can easily book 1-on-1 parent-teacher conferences with date, time, and custom agenda. Teachers can confirm, reschedule, or cancel.

Announcements & Events

Real-time campus notices, holiday circulars, sports day itineraries, and exam timetable broadcasts directly on your home feed.
